Core Technical Pillars

The Phone is the Sovereign Oracle

Eliminating centralized oracles, web APIs, and human cheat vectors through on-device hardware telemetry and Solana smart contracts.

Hardware Telemetry Oracle

Steps and physical exertion are read directly from Android SensorManager and Health Connect hardware pedometers. Digital detox habits sample Linux socket metrics via NetworkStatsManager. No web servers in between.

Seed Vault & MWA 2.0

Production integration with Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0 and Seed Vault. Private keys never leave the secure hardware enclave. User commitments and stake deposits are signed with biometric authorization.

Zero-Profit Slashing

When a user forfeits a day, nobody profits. There are no developer fees taken from discipline failure. Forfeited tokens are directly sent to spl_token::instruction::burn, permanently contracting circulating supply.

Ephemeral Session Keys

Daily clock-in should be a sub-second ritual, not an obstacle course of repetitive wallet dialogs. An isolated Ed25519 session key stored in Android Keystore authorizes daily progress in under 400 milliseconds.

The Boz Philosophy

Inspired by the legendary Mountain Goat (Boz) of rugged alpine peaks. Sure-footed balance, unyielding stamina, and zero excuses. bozPledge converts ephemeral motivation into immutable blockchain reality.

Soulbound Proof-of-Action cNFTs

Successful streaks mint compressed NFTs (cNFTs) via Bubblegum into the user's wallet. Non-transferable on-chain proof of physical endurance, verifiable by DAOs and Web3 communities.

Hackathon Evaluation

60-Second Judge Walkthrough

We built an integrated Judge Evaluation Lab directly into the Android app so evaluators can test a complete multi-day cycle in under 1 minute.

01

Launch with 1-Click Keypair

Open the app and tap "Instant Judge Devnet Keypair". Confirm the dialog to spawn a pre-funded Ed25519 keypair loaded with 2,500 $SKR on Devnet.

02

Clock In Today (Day 1)

Tap the large circular "CLOCK IN" button. The app signs instantly with the local session key, verifying Day 1 with a green checkmark and haptic feedback.

03

Open Judge Lab Sheet

Tap the "Judge Lab" pill in the top bar. Choose "Trigger Slashing Breach (Missed Day)" or "Advance Time Machine (+1 Day)" to simulate Day 2.

04

Verify On-Chain Settle

Navigate to the Vault tab to inspect the remaining locked escrow and tap "Solscan / Explorer" to audit the real-time burning transaction.
